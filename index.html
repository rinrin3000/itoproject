📲 Moving to a new phone, or just keeping a safety copy:
1. On the old device, tap ぜんぶコピー. Everything from every app on this site is copied as one piece of text — your 文型の糸 SRS schedule and your 試験の糸 ★ list together. Send it to yourself: message, email, notes app, anywhere.
2. On the new device, open this same page → 貼り付け → paste → done.

Pasting MERGES. An older backup can add things back but will never wipe newer progress, so importing the wrong copy can't cost you anything.

Each app also has its own copy/paste inside its 設定 tab — this page just does both at once so nothing gets forgotten. Progress lives only in the browser that made it: it survives app updates on its own, but not a cleared browser or a new phone.
